A local desktop privacy gateway for AI workflows — intercepts sensitive data leaks at the clipboard and prompt level, pseudonymizes them with typed tokens, proxies sanitized requests to any upstream LLM provider through secure OS keychain-backed credentials, and rehydrates responses transparently.
